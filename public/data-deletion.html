Delete my data & revoke access

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Frame AI keeps your work in your own Google Drive and nothing on our servers, so you are in full control. Pick what you want to do:

A. Delete one playlist (inside the app)

  1. Open Frame AI and press Connect Google Drive.
  2. On Playlists, press Delete on the playlist and confirm.

This permanently deletes that playlist’s folder in your Drive with its sheet, thumbnails and assets.

B. Delete everything Frame AI created

  1. Open Google Drive.
  2. Find the folder named Frame AI (also “Frame AI Config” in older versions).
  3. Move it to Trash, then empty the Trash.

This also removes the settings file that holds any API keys, YouTube connection and Buffer keys you saved.

C. Revoke Frame AI’s access to your Google account

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions (the Google security settings page).
  2. Choose Frame AI and press Delete all connections / Remove access.

After this, Frame AI and any refresh token it created can no longer reach your Drive or your YouTube channel. If you used a different Google account for your YouTube channel, repeat this for that account.

D. Other places a token may exist

  • Video backend: if you pasted a GOOGLE_REFRESH_TOKEN into your own GitHub repository, delete that secret.
  • Buffer or Gemini keys: delete or regenerate them in Buffer / Google AI Studio.
