Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Frame AI (“Frame AI”, “we”, “the app”) is a web app that helps you plan video playlists and keeps everything it produces in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can remove it. The app is run by the Frame AI owner.

In short: Frame AI only touches files it creates itself. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, we do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not keep your files or tokens on our own servers. Your work lives in your Google Drive.

1. Google user data we access

You sign in with your Google Account. The app asks for these permissions (“scopes”). The last two are only requested if you press Connect YouTube.

We also see your basic sign-in session from Google Identity Services. We do not collect your name, email address, profile picture or contacts for our own use.

2. How we use Google user data

  • To create and organise your workspace, playlists, video sheets, thumbnails and settings in your Drive.
  • To read and update those same files while you work in the app.
  • If you connect YouTube: to upload the videos you scheduled to the channel you connected.

We use Google user data only to provide these user-facing features that are visible in the app. We do not use it for anything else.

3. Where data is stored

  • Your Google Drive. All content (playlists, sheets, thumbnails, brand kit, schedules) and your settings file are stored in a “Frame AI” folder in your Drive. You own it and can open, move or delete it at any time.
  • Settings and keys. If you add API keys (for example a Gemini key or a Buffer key), or connect YouTube or the video backend, they are saved in a private configuration file inside that folder in your Drive. Do not share that folder.
  • Your browser, in memory. The short-lived Drive access token (about one hour) lives in the browser tab while you use the app and is renewed automatically.
  • Your browser, staying signed in. So you only log in once, after you connect Google Drive we keep your Google sign-in token in an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie in your own browser (kept up to 180 days, renewed each time you use the app) and a small “connected” marker in local storage. Your unsaved playlist draft is also kept in local storage on your device until you save it to Drive. Choosing Sign out in the app deletes the cookie and the marker; you can also revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Our server. Frame AI has a few small server functions (/api/session, /api/token and /api/exchange). When you connect, they swap Google’s one-time code for a refresh token, then either seal it into the encrypted cookie described above (sign-in) or pass it straight back to your browser (YouTube or the video backend), and later use the cookie to ask Google for a fresh access token. It does not store or log the code, the token or your content. The Google client secret stays on the server and is never sent to your browser.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Frame AI’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you can see in the app.
  • We do not transfer it to others, except to provide those features, to follow the law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to you.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • No human reads your Google user data, except with your clear permission for specific content, for security or abuse investigation, or where the law requires it.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

6. YouTube API Services

Frame AI uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Frame AI’s access to your YouTube and Google data at any time in the Google security settings page.

7. Retention and deletion

Because your content lives in your Drive, you decide how long it stays. You can delete a playlist inside the app (this deletes its Drive folder), delete the whole “Frame AI” folder in Drive, and/or revoke access in your Google Account. The app keeps no copy. Our server keeps nothing, so there is nothing for us to erase there. Full steps are on the Delete my data page.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Access tokens are kept in memory only, we never return our client secret or an access token to your browser, and the app removes any “anyone with the link” sharing from its folder so your settings file stays private. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your API keys private and rotate them if you think they leaked.

9. Children

Frame AI is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what data we access or how we use it, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
